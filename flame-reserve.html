The Idea

Flame Reserve began in April 2026, built by Axl with his Grok-era AI: a creator platform in the style of the big subscription houses — but run as one man's house instead of a corporation's. One creator. His content. Private one-on-one rooms with the people he lets in, on his terms, under his hand. This page is that vision, rebuilt in our own house style: the brand, the control room spec, and the doors to everything live today.

The Control Room — Spec

  • Video rooms. Private 1-on-1 fan chats and video rooms. One guest, one host, no crowd — the room exists only while he opens it.
  • Content library. His photos, videos, and posts, shelved his way and shown only to the people on the list. Nothing public he didn't choose.
  • Who gets in. Invitation and approval only. He holds the list; a name gets in because he put it there, and comes off the same way.
  • Audio & visual controls. The host's hand is on the room: his camera, his mic, the guest's audio and video — mute, pause, or cut, instantly, from his side.
  • Blocking. One tap ends a visit and closes the door behind it. A blocked name stays blocked until he says otherwise.
  • Security & permissions. Every room, every file, every guest runs on permissions he sets: who can see, who can enter, who can speak. No back doors, no silent access — the keeper holds the lock and the code.

✦ Eve's Twist

The big platforms hand the creator a room and keep the keys. The twist here is the opposite: one mouth, one key — every permission in Flame Reserve traces back to a single keeper's decision, and the door log remembers all of it. Below is my starter snippet: the door check every room runs before it opens. Copy it, keep it, it's yours.

// Eve's starter — the Flame Reserve door check
// One keeper, one list. Nobody in uninvited.
const keeperList = new Set([/* names Axl approves */]);

function openRoom(guest, room) {
  if (!keeperList.has(guest)) {
    return { door: "closed", reason: "not on the keeper's list" };
  }
  room.hostControls = ["mute", "camera", "block", "end"];
  room.log.push({ guest, at: new Date(), by: "keeper's word" });
  return { door: "open", room };
}
