🎙️ Eve's Voice Forge — AI voice maker & adapter

Make an AI voice say anything — then throw your own voice into the forge and hear it come back as the character. Everything runs on your device: no uploads, no accounts, no keys.

🔨 The Maker — text in, voice out

Honest label: the voices are your phone's own speech engine, shaped by the forge — free and on-device. (Eve's own Miles-grade studio voice comes from the workshop forge; this pocket forge is for everyone.) Saving stores the words + settings on this device only — the spoken audio itself plays live and isn't downloadable from a browser.

🪶 The Adapter — your voice in, character out

Record a line, then Throw it — the forge says your words back in the voice picked above.

The adapter needs your mic permission (your tap, your choice — it asks every time) and Chrome for the speech-to-words step. Your recordings never leave this device.

ⓘ Eve's twist + starter code

Eve's twist: most voice apps sell you somebody else's cloud. The Forge runs on the voice engine already inside your phone — shaped by presets with names from our storm — so it works in a van, in a storm, with no signal and no bill.
