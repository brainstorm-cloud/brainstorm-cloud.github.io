🦉 Night Owl Maps

The layout you already know — the data nobody owns. Same shape as the big maps: search on top, the world underneath, tap and fly. Underneath ours is OpenStreetMap — the open map of the world, drawn by people, free for everyone, forever.

Eve's twist: directions aren't a line you follow — they're a flight. Hit ➤ or "Fly there" and an owl rides the route, the way we'd actually travel it. Pins are birds. Your perch is an owl. The night layer is home.

Search runs on the community Nominatim service, flights are routed by the open OSRM engine, and the sky layer is Esri imagery. No account, no sign-in, nothing of yours stored — the map forgets you the way our storm forgets files.

Starter code — the whole trick in one line:

map.flyTo([lat, lon], 15, { duration: 2.2 }); // the bird-smooth glide
